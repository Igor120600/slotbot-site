Which Visa Centre Handles Greek Visas?

Greek visa applications in the UK are processed by VFS Global. Available locations:

  • London
  • Manchester
  • Birmingham

Who Needs a Greek Visa?

Non-EEA nationals residing in the UK with a BRP card need a Schengen visa to visit Greece. British citizens do not require a visa for short stays under 90 days. Common nationalities applying include Indian, Pakistani, Bangladeshi, Nigerian, Filipino and Chinese nationals.

Required Documents

  1. Completed visa application form — via VFS Global portal
  2. Valid passport — valid 3+ months after return date, minimum 2 blank pages
  3. 2 passport photos — 35×45mm biometric
  4. Travel insurance — minimum €30,000 cover for all Schengen countries
  5. Proof of accommodation — hotel confirmation or host letter
  6. Flight itinerary — return flights booked or provisional
  7. Bank statements — last 3 months showing sufficient funds
  8. Proof of employment — employer letter, payslips or contract
  9. UK residence proof — BRP or visa vignette
  10. Cover letter — explaining purpose and itinerary
