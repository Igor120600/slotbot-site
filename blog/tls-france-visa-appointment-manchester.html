TLS France visa appointment Manchester

📅 July 6, 2026 ⏱ 6 min read ✍️ SlotBot Team

If you're in the North West and typing "TLS France visa appointment Manchester" into Google over and over, you already know the frustration: you log in, the calendar is empty, you refresh, still nothing. Manchester is one of only three TLScontact centres handling France visas in the UK, and the demand for those slots is brutal. Here's how the Manchester centre actually works, why it's so hard to book, and what genuinely helps you get in.

First, the good news about Manchester

France visa applications in the UK are handled by TLScontact, and there are three centres: London, Manchester and Edinburgh. The important part most people don't realise: there is no consular area. You are not tied to your nearest centre. Any UK resident can apply at whichever centre has a free slot.

That cuts both ways. London is the busiest and its slots vanish fastest — so if you live near Manchester, your local centre may actually be easier to book than London. And if Manchester is full, you're allowed to grab a London or Edinburgh slot instead. More on that trick below.

📍 Where the Manchester centre is

The TLScontact France visa centre sits in Manchester city centre, close to Piccadilly Gardens — easy to reach by tram or train. Always double-check the exact address on the official TLScontact site before you travel, as it can change.

Why there are "no appointments" in Manchester

This is the part that drives people mad. It's not that appointments don't exist — it's that they're gone before you can click. A few reasons:

  • Slots drop in batches. TLScontact releases appointments in waves, not continuously. If you're not looking at the exact moment a batch drops, you miss it.
  • Cancellations reappear randomly. Someone cancels, a slot pops back up — and it's taken within seconds by whoever happens to be refreshing right then.
  • Summer is chaos. For popular travel dates, slots can disappear in under a minute across all UK centres.
  • Everyone's watching the same page. Manual refreshing means you're racing hundreds of other applicants by hand.

None of this means Manchester is impossible. It means you have to be watching at the right second — which is exactly the thing humans are bad at and software is good at.

The Manchester-specific trick: don't only watch Manchester

Because there's no residency restriction, the smartest applicants in the North West don't stare at a single Manchester calendar. They watch Manchester, London and Edinburgh at the same time. A slot in Edinburgh two weeks from now, plus a cheap train, often beats waiting a month for Manchester to open up.

Think of it as widening the net: watching all three centres roughly triples your chances of catching an early opening. If your travel date is close, this flexibility can be the difference between flying on time and missing the trip entirely.

Timing: book earlier than you think

Two numbers worth remembering. You can apply up to 180 days before your travel dates, and you should allow at least 20 working days for processing. Translation: the moment your dates are roughly fixed, start hunting for a slot. Waiting until a month before departure is how people end up stuck.

⚠️ Watch out for scams

TLScontact is the only provider authorised by the French Consulate for UK France visas. Ignore any website claiming to sell you an "official" appointment. Booking itself is done through the official system — legitimate help is about finding a slot, not selling you a fake one.

What actually helps you get a Manchester slot

Realistically, you have three options:

  1. Refresh manually. Free, but you'd need to watch the page around the clock — and you'll still lose most races to faster clickers.
  2. Check at odd hours. Slots sometimes drop early morning; worth a try, but it's a lottery.
  3. Use a monitoring service. A tool that watches all three UK centres every minute and alerts you (or books automatically) the instant a slot appears. This is what removes the round-the-clock refreshing.
