What is SlotBot? The Schengen Visa Appointment Service for UK Residents

📅 June 24, 2026 ⏱ 5 min read ✍️ SlotBot Team

If you've tried to book a Schengen visa appointment in the UK recently, you already know how frustrating it is. TLSContact and VFS Global calendars are almost always fully booked — and the rare cancellation slots disappear within seconds. SlotBot was built to solve exactly this problem.

What is SlotBot?

SlotBot (slotbot.uk) is a UK-based Schengen visa appointment booking and monitoring service. It works by checking visa centre availability every 60 seconds — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — and either sending you an instant notification the moment a slot appears, or booking it automatically on your behalf using your TLSContact or VFS Global account.

SlotBot operates via a Telegram bot (@slotbot_visa_bot) and covers 24 Schengen countries from the UK and UAE. Most clients get a confirmed appointment within 1–2 days of activating the service.

Why Was SlotBot Created?

Getting a Schengen visa appointment in the UK has become genuinely difficult. France, Germany, Italy and Spain are among the most popular destinations — and their visa centres in London, Manchester and Birmingham are overwhelmed with demand. Slots fill up weeks in advance, and the only way to catch a cancellation is to refresh the booking page constantly.

Most people either give up after days of manual refreshing, miss the slot by a few seconds, or pay expensive visa agents with no guarantee of success. SlotBot was created to give people a reliable, affordable alternative — automated monitoring that works even while you sleep.

How SlotBot Works

  1. Choose your service — Notifications (you book manually when alerted) or Autobooking (SlotBot books for you automatically)
  2. Select your details — application country, city, destination country, visa category and date window
  3. Pay once — secure payment via Stripe, no subscription
  4. SlotBot starts monitoring — checks every 60 seconds across all UK cities simultaneously
  5. Slot found — you get an instant Telegram notification, or SlotBot books it automatically

Who Is SlotBot For?

SlotBot is designed for anyone living in the UK or UAE who needs a Schengen visa appointment and can't find an available slot. This includes:

  • People on BRP cards (Indian, Pakistani, Bangladeshi, Nigerian, Filipino, Chinese and other nationals)
  • Holders of UK refugee travel documents who need a visa for specific countries
  • Families applying for multiple applicants
  • Anyone who has been trying to get a slot manually for days or weeks with no success

Is SlotBot Safe?

Yes. SlotBot uses your TLSContact or VFS Global login credentials only to complete the booking on your behalf — credentials are transmitted securely and are never stored after the booking is made. Payments are processed by Stripe, one of the most trusted payment processors in the world. SlotBot does not store card details.
