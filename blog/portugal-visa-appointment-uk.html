VFS Global Portugal appointments in London fill up quickly, especially during school holidays. Slots from cancellations appear without warning and are taken within minutes.

Which Visa Centre Handles Portuguese Visas?

Portuguese Schengen visa applications in the UK are processed by VFS Global at these locations:

  • London

You can apply at any location regardless of where you live in the UK.

Who Needs a Portuguese Visa?

Non-EEA nationals living in the UK need a Schengen visa to visit Portugal. This applies to most people holding a BRP card, including Indian, Pakistani, Bangladeshi, Nigerian, Filipino, Chinese and other nationalities. British citizens do not need a visa for stays under 90 days.

Required Documents

  1. Completed application form — filled via the VFS Global portal
  2. Valid passport — valid at least 3 months after your return date, with 2 blank pages
  3. 2 biometric passport photos — 35×45mm, white background
  4. Travel insurance — minimum €30,000, valid across all Schengen countries
  5. Accommodation proof — hotel bookings or invitation from a host in Portugal
  6. Return flight itinerary
  7. Bank statements — last 3–6 months showing sufficient funds (minimum ~€50 per day)
  8. Proof of employment — employer letter, contract or payslips
  9. UK residence proof — BRP, visa vignette or settled status document
  10. Cover letter — explaining purpose of visit and itinerary

Processing Time

The Portuguese consulate typically takes 15 working days to process a Schengen visa. Apply at least 4–6 weeks before your travel date. During peak season (summer and Christmas) processing can take longer.

Step-by-Step Application Guide

  1. Register on the VFS Global Portugal UK portal and fill in your application
  2. Prepare all required documents
  3. Use SlotBot to monitor and secure an appointment — slots fill fast
  4. Attend your appointment and submit biometrics
  5. Track your application via VFS Global SMS/email updates
  6. Collect your passport with visa (or check courier delivery option)
💡 Pro tip: Apply for the right country

If you are visiting multiple Schengen countries, apply for a visa from the country where you will spend the most days, or your first entry point. Portugal is often chosen because processing is reliable and slots (when available) open up regularly.

Tips for a Successful Portuguese Visa Application

  • Apply as early as possible — up to 6 months before travel
  • Ensure travel insurance explicitly covers all Schengen countries
  • Show consistent bank balance — not just a recent large deposit
  • Do not book non-refundable flights until your visa is approved
