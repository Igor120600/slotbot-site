How to Book an Italy Visa Appointment in the UK in 2026

📅 June 15, 2026⏱ 5 min read✍️ SlotBot Team

Getting an Italian Schengen visa appointment in the UK has become increasingly difficult in 2026. VFS Global — the official visa centre for Italy — often shows fully booked calendars for weeks ahead. This guide walks you through everything you need to know.

⚠️ Italy slots are very competitive in summer

During peak travel season (May–September), Italian visa slots in London disappear within minutes of appearing. Start your application as early as possible — you can apply up to 6 months before travel.

Which Visa Centre Handles Italian Visas?

Italian visa applications in the UK are handled by VFS Global. Locations:

  • London — main centre, highest volume
  • Manchester — serves North England
  • Birmingham — serves the Midlands
  • Edinburgh — serves Scotland

You can apply at any UK centre regardless of where you live.

Who Needs an Italian Visa from the UK?

Non-EEA nationals residing in the UK need a Schengen visa to visit Italy. This includes most people on BRP cards — Indian, Pakistani, Bangladeshi, Nigerian, Filipino, Chinese nationals and many others.

British citizens do not need a visa for stays under 90 days.

Documents Required

  1. Completed application form — filled via the VFS Global portal
  2. Valid passport — valid at least 3 months after your return date
  3. 2 biometric photos — 35×45mm, white background
  4. Travel insurance — minimum €30,000, covering all Schengen countries
  5. Hotel bookings or invitation letter
  6. Return flight itinerary
  7. Bank statements — last 3–6 months
  8. Proof of employment — employer letter or contract
  9. UK residence proof — BRP, visa vignette or settled status
  10. Cover letter explaining your trip

Processing Time

The Italian consulate typically takes 15 working days to process a visa. During peak summer months this can extend to 3–4 weeks. Always apply well in advance — ideally 6–8 weeks before travel.

Italy is consistently one of the most popular Schengen destinations. VFS Global centres are overwhelmed with demand, and available slots — often from cancellations — appear and disappear within seconds. Many applicants spend days manually refreshing the booking page with no success.
