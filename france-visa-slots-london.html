Who Needs a French Visa from the UK?

You need a French Schengen visa if you are a non-EEA national residing in the UK and plan to visit France for tourism, business, family visits, or study. British citizens do not require a visa for stays under 90 days.

Common nationalities applying from the UK include: Indian, Pakistani, Bangladeshi, Nigerian, Ghanaian, Filipino, Chinese, and many others on BRP cards.

Which Visa Centre Handles French Visas?

French visa applications in the UK are handled exclusively by TLSContact. Locations:

  • London — main centre, highest volume
  • Manchester — serves the North of England
  • Birmingham — serves the Midlands
  • Edinburgh — serves Scotland

You can apply at any of these centres regardless of where you live in the UK.

Documents for a French Schengen Visa

  • Signed application form (completed online via TLSContact)
  • Valid passport (valid 3+ months after return date, with 2 blank pages)
  • 2 passport photos (35mm × 45mm, white background)
  • Travel insurance (min €30,000, covering all Schengen countries)
  • Proof of accommodation in France (hotel or host letter)
  • Flight itinerary (confirmed or provisional)
  • Bank statements (last 3 months, showing sufficient funds)
  • Cover letter explaining purpose of visit
  • Employment proof (employer letter, payslips, or contract)
  • UK residence proof (BRP, visa, or settled status)

How Long Will It Take?

French consulates typically process visa applications within 15 working days. Apply at least 3–4 weeks before your trip. During summer and school holiday periods, processing can take longer.

💡 Pro tip: Apply earlier than you think

You can apply up to 6 months before your trip. The earlier you apply, the more likely you are to find an appointment slot — and the more time you have if something goes wrong.
