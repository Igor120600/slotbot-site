1. Who We Are

SlotBot is a Schengen visa appointment monitoring and booking service. Contact: botforslot@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide the service:

  • Telegram user ID and username
  • Email address (for notifications)
  • TLSContact account email and password (for autobooking only)
  • Selected visa destination, category, and date preferences

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To monitor visa centre availability on your behalf
  • To book an appointment using your TLSContact account

We do not sell, share, or use your data for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention (GDPR)

We are GDPR compliant. Your personal data — including TLSContact credentials — is deleted automatically once your appointment has been successfully booked or your subscription has expired. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

5. Data Security

All data is stored securely. Passwords are encrypted and never displayed in plain text. We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information.
