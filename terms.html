1. About SlotBot

SlotBot is a service that monitors visa centre availability and assists users in booking appointments at Schengen visa centres. SlotBot is not affiliated with any official visa centre, embassy, or consulate.

2. Service Description

SlotBot offers two services:

  • Notifications — alerts sent via Telegram when an appointment slot becomes available at a selected visa centre.
  • Autobooking — automatic reservation of an appointment slot on behalf of the user using their TLSContact account credentials.

3. User Responsibilities

By using SlotBot, you confirm that:

  • You have an active TLSContact account with a completed visa application for the selected destination.
  • You are responsible for maintaining your account in active status.
  • You provide accurate credentials and contact information.
  • You understand that SlotBot can only book an appointment if your application is valid and active.
