Which Countries Require a Schengen Visa?

As a UK resident (non-British citizen or British citizen travelling to certain countries), you may need a Schengen visa to visit the following countries:

  • France, Germany, Spain, Italy, Netherlands
  • Portugal, Greece, Austria, Belgium, Switzerland
  • Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland and 14 more countries

British citizens do not need a Schengen visa for stays under 90 days. However, non-British residents living in the UK (including those on BRP cards) typically do need one.

Where to Apply in the UK

Depending on your destination country, you'll apply at either TLSContact or VFS Global. Both operate visa application centres in:

  • 🏙 London
  • 🏙 Manchester
  • 🏙 Birmingham
  • 🏙 Edinburgh
  • 🏙 Cardiff

For example, French visa applications go through TLSContact, while German applications typically go through VFS Global.

Documents You'll Need

While exact requirements vary by country, the standard Schengen visa document list includes:

  1. Completed application form — filled online via the visa centre's portal
  2. Valid passport — must be valid for at least 3 months beyond your planned return date
  3. Passport photos — typically 2 recent biometric photos
  4. Travel insurance — minimum €30,000 coverage, valid in all Schengen countries
  5. Proof of accommodation — hotel booking or invitation letter
  6. Proof of financial means — bank statements for the last 3–6 months
  7. Flight itinerary — confirmed or provisional bookings
  8. Cover letter — explaining the purpose of your trip
  9. Proof of employment or studies — employer letter, contract, or student ID
  10. Biometric residence permit (BRP) — if you're a non-British resident
