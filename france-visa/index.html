France Schengen Visa Overview for UK Residents

If you are a non-EEA national residing in the UK (most people on a BRP card), you need a French Schengen visa to enter France for tourism, business, or short-term visits.

British citizens and EU/EEA nationals do not require a visa for short stays under 90 days.

✈️ Key fact: France is the most popular Schengen destination

France receives more Schengen visa applications from the UK than almost any other country. This makes appointment availability extremely competitive — slots fill within minutes of appearing.

Entry Requirements for France

  • Passport: Valid for at least 3 months after your return date, with at least 2 blank pages
  • Travel Insurance: Minimum €30,000 Schengen cover (covers all Schengen countries)
  • Proof of Funds: Minimum €120/day or €65/day with prepaid accommodation
  • Accommodation: Hotel booking, Airbnb confirmation, or signed invitation letter from a host in France
  • Return Transport: Confirmed or provisional flight itinerary
  • UK Residence Proof: BRP card, visa vignette, or settled/pre-settled status letter

France Visa FAQs

Do I need a visa if I'm a British citizen?

No. British citizens can stay in France for up to 90 days without a visa.

How long does a French visa take?

Standard processing is 15 working days. In peak season (summer), add 5-10 extra days. Always apply 4-6 weeks in advance.

Can I apply in Manchester or Birmingham instead of London?

Yes. You can apply at any UK TLSContact centre regardless of where you live. Manchester and Birmingham often have better availability.

What if my application is rejected?

You can appeal or reapply. Common rejection reasons: insufficient funds, weak travel insurance, or incomplete documentation. Fix these and try again.

Can my visa be extended?

Schengen tourist visas cannot be extended. You must return to the UK before expiry. If you need a longer stay, apply for a long-stay visa (D) instead.
